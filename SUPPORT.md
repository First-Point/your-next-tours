# Support for Your Next Tours

## Report a problem or send feedback

Use the feedback form. It goes straight to the team and lets you attach a screenshot:

https://yournext.tours/contact

You can also email contact@firstpoint.com.tr.

## Questions and suggestions

Open an issue in this repository. Issues are for questions about how Your Next Tours works
and for feature suggestions. For anything that needs device details or a screenshot, the
feedback form above is the better channel.

## Privacy

Privacy policy: https://yournext.tours/privacy-policy
Terms: https://yournext.tours/terms-of-use
Guides can also reach the team from the guide panel at https://yournext.tours.
